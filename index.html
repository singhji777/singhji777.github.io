Public datasetChurn

Telco Customer Churn and Retention ROI

Predicting who will leave is half the job. This project decides who to contact by expected profit, and shows where the campaign stops paying off.

  • Targeting by expected profit earns about USD 2,500 more per 1,000 customers than the best simple rule (95% range 1,589 to 3,487).
  • Month-to-month customers are 55% of the base but 89% of those who leave.
  • Model scores ROC-AUC 0.84 on 7,043 customers.
Python · scikit-learn · statsmodels
